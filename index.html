How the lock works

  • Each station has 2–4 questions. Each answer becomes part of that station's code.
  • Press Run diagnostics to test the code. The station tells you how many readings are stable, but not which ones.

Hint cards

  • Every station has two sealed hint cards.
  • Opening a card costs 2 minutes of oxygen (your teacher can switch this off).
  • Talk it through as a team before you open one.

Rules of the station

  • Stations unlock in order, 1 to 6.
  • Use only the evidence on screen, your notes and a calculator.
  • Every station has a Field Log question. Your teacher may collect it, so write a full scientific answer.

Growth rate (%) = (CBR − CDR) ÷ 10, because the rates are per 1,000. Doubling time ≈ 70 ÷ growth rate (%). A country where death rates have fallen but birth rates are still high is in the stage of fastest growth.
On a log axis, a Type II curve is a straight diagonal (a constant mortality rate). Type III drops steeply at the start. K is the level the herd keeps oscillating around after the crash, not the peak.
