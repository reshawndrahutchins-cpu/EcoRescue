# Eco-Rescue: Escape the Environmental Crisis

**▶ Play it: https://reshawndrahutchins-cpu.github.io/EcoRescue/**

![Eco-Rescue start screen](assets/preview.png)

A 45–60 minute team escape room for **AP Environmental Science** and **Cambridge Environmental Management**. A solar storm has knocked out six life-support systems in a global research station. Teams of 3–4 environmental scientists analyze real-style data to restore each system, recover six key fragments, and open the emergency exit before the countdown ends.

## The six stations

| # | System | Science |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1 | Population Projection Core | Demographic transition, rule of 70, survivorship curves, carrying capacity |
| 2 | Kelp Forest Biodome | Food webs, keystone species, ecosystem services, invasive species, habitat fragmentation |
| 3 | Riverbend Hydrology Bay | Watersheds, point vs. nonpoint pollution, eutrophication, aquifers, irrigation |
| 4 | Power & Climate Core | Renewable vs. nonrenewable energy, carbon cycle, Keeling curve, GWP, mitigation |
| 5 | Toxicology & Waste Lab | Biomagnification, primary vs. secondary air pollutants, environmental justice, solid waste |
| 6 | Command Deck | Scoring four community proposals with a sustainability rubric |

Each station has a mission briefing, evidence (graphs, maps, data tables, clue cards), 2–4 questions whose answers form the station code, two hint cards, and a written Field Log question. It ends with a team debrief: *"Which environmental decisions would have the greatest long-term impact on protecting our planet, and why?"*

## Running it in class

1. Give each team **one device** (Chromebook, laptop or tablet) and open the link above.
2. Assign roles: **Mission Commander**, **Data Analyst**, **Field Scientist**, **Systems Engineer**. With three students, the Commander also acts as the Systems Engineer.
3. Teams pick 45, 50, 55 or 60 minutes and press **Begin mission**.
4. Each hint card opened takes 2 minutes off the clock (this can be switched off).
5. Progress saves on each device, so a refresh won't lose a team's work.

**Sound:** the page plays generated music and sound effects. Use the **Music**, **SFX** and volume controls in the top bar, or have teams use headphones.

**Paper version:** download the [student packet (PDF)](student-packet.pdf). It includes a Mission Log and cut-out hint cards.

## For teachers

The **Teacher** button (top right) opens a console with the full answer key, explanations and sample Field Log answers. It also lets you unlock all stations, add or remove time, pause the clock, turn the hint penalty on or off, reset a device, and print the student packet.

The answer key is **encrypted with the teacher PIN**, so it can't be read from this repository or the page's code. The PIN is in your private teacher notes, not here. Station answers are checked against one-way hashes rather than stored in the page.

## Privacy and accessibility

- No accounts, tracking or data collection. Progress stays in each browser's local storage.
- Readable fonts (Atkinson Hyperlegible), high-contrast evidence cards and full keyboard support.
- Respects **reduced motion** settings: animations switch off and everything still works.
- Works on current Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, and on phones down to 360 px wide.
